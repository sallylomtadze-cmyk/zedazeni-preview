The road · 2011–2026

01 / 15
  1. 2011

    Founded

    Georgian Beer Company was founded by Tsezar Chocheli's business group. An investment of 80 million lari, 100% Georgian capital.

  2. 2012

    The plant opens

    On 2 April a European-standard plant opened in Saguramo. Two flagship products reached the market: Zedazeni beer and Zedazeni lemonade.

  3. 2013

    Wilder

    The first Georgian energy drink.

  4. 2014

    Khevsuruli

    The traditional Georgian beer Khevsuruli and the lager Okros Artsivi (Golden Eagle).

  5. 2015

    Baikho

    The first Georgian iced tea.

  6. 2016

    RC Cola

    Production of RC Cola begins in Georgia under licence from Royal Crown Cola International.

  7. 2017

    Chero

    The first juice in the region made on an aseptic line. Investment in the project exceeds 25 million lari.

  8. 2018

    Ragnar

    The Scandinavian Ragnar Pilsner, with Dolph Lundgren. Holsten, a Carlsberg Group brand.

  9. 2019

    HARP and Zeni

    HARP under licence from Diageo. Gurieli iced tea, the energy drink Dino and Zeni spring water.

  10. 2020

    Aia returns

    The first Georgian beer brand, created in 1953, is on sale again. Ravi comes to market.

  11. 2021

    Tsiv-Tsivi

    Live draught beer. Exports exceed the pre-pandemic level by 34%.

  12. 2022

    Miller

    MILLER is brewed in Saguramo under licence from Molson Coors. Dilmah iced tea joins the portfolio.

  13. 2024

    Daily Group

    The company became part of Georgia's largest trading holding.

  14. 2025

    Heineken

    On 22 May Tempo Beverages acquired a 60% stake. Since 1 July Zedazeni has been Heineken's exclusive representative. By the end of the year it leads the beer market.

  15. 2026

    A new chapter

    Since 18 March the company has been led by Giorgi Kesanashvili.

How it began

Eight
months

Georgian Beer Company was founded in 2011 by Tsezar Chocheli's business group. It was the group's third beer and lemonade plant in Georgia. The project was a risky, bold step, yet exactly eight months after construction began the plant's products were waiting for customers in shops across the country.

  1. 150 m

    Groundwater

    At the foot of Mount Zedazeni, 150 metres down, water gathers between layers of rock.

  2. The way up

    Our own wells

    The water rises to the surface through the company's own wells.

  3. 0 m

    The Saguramo plant

    At the surface the water flows straight into the plant and becomes the base of our beer and lemonade.
