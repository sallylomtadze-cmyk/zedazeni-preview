Production

Spread across a vast site, the plant produces both alcoholic and non-alcoholic drinks. Throughout the entire cycle, raw materials and product touch nothing but the highest-grade stainless steel.

Quality

Proud quality
at every stage

Products are checked at every stage of production: from the selection of raw materials and laboratory testing through to filling, packaging and warehousing.

01

Filling lines

Glass and PET bottles, aluminium cans and metal kegs. German lines from KHS and Krones, where electronic production control rules out human error.

02

Zedazeni water

The site was chosen on the strength of geological surveys. The company draws water from its own wells, 150 metres down.

03

Laboratory

Chemical and microbiological control at every stage, from raw materials to finished product. A sample from every filling run is kept in the laboratory.

04

Licensor control

A sample from every batch of a licensed product is sent to the licensor's laboratory, and the batch reaches the shops only after its approval.

05

Aseptic technology

Cold processing of the fruit preserves vitamins, flavour and colour with no additives. The first aseptic line in the region.

06

FSSC certification

The company has implemented international food-safety standards. Certification and regular monitoring are carried out by Germany's TÜV SÜD.
