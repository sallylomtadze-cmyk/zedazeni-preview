Georgia's leading producer of beer and soft drinks. In Saguramo, at the foot of Mount Zedazeni, since 2011.

Our mission

To create products Georgia can be proud of. To preserve, strengthen and develop Georgian traditions.

In its concept, design, production equipment and management systems, the company is an innovation in the soft drinks and beer industry.

The portfolio brings together local and licensed beers, lemonade, cola, energy drinks, spring water, flavoured water and iced tea.
